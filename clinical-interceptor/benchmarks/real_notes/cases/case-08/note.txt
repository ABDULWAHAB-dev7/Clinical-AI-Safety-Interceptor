URGENT CARE NOTE

HPI: A 35-year-old woman presents with sore throat, fever, and tender cervical nodes for two days. She can swallow liquids without difficulty.

Exam: Temperature 38.4 C. Tonsils are swollen with exudate. No rash.

Assessment: Probable streptococcal pharyngitis.

Plan: Acetaminophen for fever.
Start amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily.
Return if breathing becomes difficult or the fever persists beyond three days.
